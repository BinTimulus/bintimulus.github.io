The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin

Dialogue 04: - 2026 OCT 16
Vengeance.

2026 OCT 13, 12:35
Blackgrove College, Cafeteria
(Bin takes a seat opposite to Aria, already having her lunch.)

Aria:
Hi Bin!

How's your day been?

Bin:
...

Aria:
Bin?

Bin:
This is exhausting. I just- can't.

Why am I like this?

Aria:
Like what?

Bin:
(Bin buries their head in their hands, and sighs.)

Aria:
Bin, I can't do anything about it if you don't tell me.

Obviously, you don't have to - but it'd help both of us if you did.

Bin:
Alright.

You know this group project I have?

The presentation for physics?

Aria:
The one about quark compositions?

Bin:
I got a week of hard work deleted today. For a joke.

And that idiot who deleted my work just said,
'Can't take a teensy joke, Timothy? You can just write it all back from memory anyways.'

(Bin hits the table.)

I want to give him payback.

But I can't, can I? I'm neither strong enough, nor ready to face the consequences even if I was.

I just feel so bad. My work's gone, and all I can think about is revenge.

Aria:
I get it - you're frustrated.

You're not wrong for wanting retribution.

I have my fair share of people I'd like to beat up.

You want some advice?

You've problably heard enough of 'at least you care' and 'let's see who wins in the end' already, so I won't bore you with that again.

Yeah yeah - we all know it's wrong to hurt other people. But then again, if they hurt us, it's only normal to feel this way.

Bin:
I get what you mean.

Aria:
Makes more sense now, no?

Though, here's something to remind yourself with the next time you want to send it right into their face.

No, it's not the moral high ground, or even 'the greater good'. Somebody's probably lectured you all about that as well.

Leaving a crater in their face might (I mean, would most definitely) bring you a lot of satisfaction in the moment, wouldn't it?

But would it fix how you feel?

Bin:
Not so much.

Aria:
And there you have it.

They might deserve that beating, but if that doesn't give you closure, why do it?

It really wouldn't work if we all ran around beating up everybody who has ever bullied us either as well.

Bin:
A bit extreme, but true.

Aria:
Well then, are you feeling any better now that we've talked about this?

Bin:
Maybe. There's still a lot to sort through though.

Aria:
There always will be.

Bin:
I've learnt a lot from this as well.

Namely, that there's no running from trolls.

But there is figuring out how to deal with them.

Aria:
Exactly.

Bin:
Thanks for listening, Ari.

Aria:
No worries.

If I'm of any help, I'm happy to, anytime, anywhere. You matter a lot to me. Especially...

Espscially for emergency homework help.

Bin:
What, you need that now?

I'm not that good anyways.

Aria:
Come on. 'Not that good?'

Bin:
I'm being honest!

Aria:
I'll take your word for that then.

Ten minutes later...
Bin:
Well then, what now?

Aria:
Chess club?

Bin:
I thought you hated that.

Aria:
But you like chess, no?

Bin:
I wouldn't say I like chess, but I'm rather interested in the strategy.

Aria:
You know what, Bin? I have a strategy to beat you this time.

Let's go so I can test that out.

Bin:
Alright. If that's what you want.

Could be fun.
2026 OCT 13
