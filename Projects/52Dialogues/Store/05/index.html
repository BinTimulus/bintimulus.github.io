The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin

Dialogue 05: - 2026 OCT 23
Porton Cafe, Porton.

2026 OCT 23, 17:28
Porton Cafe, Porton
(Aria enters the Cafe to find Bin already there, reading a textbook.)

Aria:
Hey Bin! Whatcha reading?

Bin:
Ari. See for yourself.
(Without looking up, Bin raises their textbook up for Aria to see the title.)

Aria:
Serious?

Bin:
Can't be more so.

Here for your Friday sandwich?

Aria:
Yes I am.

(Points at Bin's own sandwich)
I see you're also having one.

Bin:
(Still reading) Mhm.
An order later...
(Aria returns and takes a seat opposite to Bin, having bought her own sandwich.)

Bin:
Welcome back.

Aria:
What's with coming here today?
(Takes a bite off her sandwich.)

Bin:
No homework, I guess.
(Again, without looking up, takes their sandwich, takes a bite, and puts it back.)

Aria:
Bin?

Bin:
Hmm?

Aria:
You do realise that you're talking to me, right?

Bin:
And?

Aria:
...

Bin:
(Finally sets the textbook on the table, and turns to look at Aria, awaiting a response.)

Yes Ari?

Aria:
You know what? Nevermind.

I shouldn't have expected anything other than this.

Bin:
(Still looking inquisitively at Aria.)

(Awkward silence.)

So...
How's your week been?

Aria:
Good. Good.

...

Not much homework on my end either.

Bin:
That's good to hear.

Aria:
...

(Silence, again.)

Bin:
Oh. I get it.

(Points at the book)
This is what you were mad about.

Aria:
(Sighs) We got there in the end.

You've got to look at people when they're trying to have a conversation with you, Bin.

Bin:
I'm not well suited for anything social then, I guess.

(Bin takes another bite out of their sandwich.)

I wonder why you still stick around sometimes.

Aria:
You're a good escape from reality sometimes.
In all respects.

That makes you a good person to hang around.

I guess.

Bin:
Thanks, Ari. You're nice to be around as well.

Aria:
Not to brag - (Must the author include "proudly, with a hint of sarcasm" to imply such?)

But of course I am!

(She takes a bite out of her sandwich.)

(Bin stares at her, blinking slowly, twice.)

Aria:
Well? Worried for your library group on Sunday?

Bin:
Maybe. I got the notes from the manager.

(Stretches) Prep work- starts- tomorrow.

Aria:
It's good that you're finally giving yourself your Friday back.

Bin:
I have enough time, after all.

How about you and work?

Aria:
I don't really feel anything about it.
It's money, and it's good experience.

Bin:
I see.

That's something for your CV too.

Hold on-

Aria:
Yeah Bin?

Bin:
I've got to go for a moment.
Look after my stuff.

Aria:
Alright.

(Takes another bite out of her sandwich.)
Five minutes later...
(Bin returns, finding Aria on her phone.)

Bin:
I'm back!

Aria:
(On her phone) Mhm.

Bin:
(Gestures at Aria's phone) See anything interesting on there?

Aria:
(Still scrolling)
Not much.

Bin:
...

Oh well. Back to reading then.

(Picks the textbook up again by one hand, picking up the last bit of their sandwich on the other.)

(Nothing much happens for a moment.)

Aria:
Bin?

Bin:
(Reading.) Yeah?

Aria:
(Sighs) Nothing.

(Uses her phone to take a picture.)
Ari_239:
Hey Bin, catch!
