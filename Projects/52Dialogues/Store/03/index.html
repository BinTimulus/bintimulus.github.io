The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin

Dialogue 03: - 2026 OCT 07
Order of Operations

2026 OCT 02, 17:30
Stone St Park, Porton
(Aria, paper bag in hand, paces up to Bin, who is already waiting for her.)

Aria:
Hey Bin.

Bin:
Hey Ari. Got your message.

Aria:
(Sits down next to Bin, and takes a sandwich out of her paper bag.)

Wondering why I invited you out here, Bin?

Bin:
I mean, you wanting to chat somewhere else really isn't that far beyond guessing, is it?

This change of scenery is nice though.

Aria:
Aaaand- Halfway there.

There's also the part where I want to just forget about all my responsibilities for a while.

Bin:
Reasonable.

I do have my fair share of homework piling up that I'd rather not think about.

Yet talking about that, should we be doing this now?

Aria:
Why not? We have the whole weekend ahead of us! Spending an hour here wouldn't hurt.

What's more - I've got work tomorrow. I think I deserve a nice rest first.

Bin:
Won't it be better to do all of your homework first though, Ari? With that you'll end up with a whole, unbroken weekend for you to do whatever you want.

I get that you might say you need some time to unwind before taking on another workload, but I'd say that the stress is a necessary evil for welcoming the weekend.

Aria:
A necessary evil that makes you look like you've been through 20 hours of relentless torture when you show up on Saturday morning, Bin, mind you.

Burning the candle on both ends just to earn a continuous bout of free time seems not that worth it. You're bound to go mad at some point from all of that.

Bin:
You know what? Perfectly fair. Maybe you do have a point with my strategy with tasks, but this isn't to say that your approach is perfectly sound. Might I remind you of what happened last Sunday with the maths homework?

Aria:
About that-

Bin:
Yes Ari, it is very unfair that we got something stretching what we have learnt already to the logical extremes, but I'd still say that you'll never know how long some tasks take, so why not do the unpredicatble stuff first, and then leave the less important leisure after?

Aria:
And that's where I think you have it wrong.

Spending some time off playing videogames or, hell, bedrotting, is just as essential to your life as doing other stuff! Personally? I'd die without that. Neither can you survive without your uncomprehensible 'reading for fun', I think.

Bin:
Now that you say this...

Yet what about procrastination? I get leaving yourself the evening off, but once you wake up the next morning, that becomes 'until lunch', and that stretches onto 'I'll do it Sunday night'. We both know that this happens, and where this leads to.

Aria:
But this is an inevitable consequence, perhaps?
From what I see, one problem leads to the next - we'll spend ages trying to find the final solution.

Bin:
I guess you're right.

In the end, we do each have our best ways of dealing with work, and our flaws to learn about. For me, I think I've got to figure out spreading my work out, and you, not putting it off until the last moment.

Aria:
Yes, and we do both have flaws in our methods, but neither of our methods, or any approach to work, I'd say, is the best. As you said, we'll have to learn from each other.

Huh.

Bin:
Yes Ari?

Aria:
We did end up learning something when we wanted to escape learning.

Ironic.

Bin:
Interesting observation.

But what's an even more interesting one? That you, in fact, have to work tomorrow.

Aria:
And you have to go teach kids how to read on Sunday.

But enough of that. Let me start by not procrastinating on my sandwich. Here. Have half of it.

Bin:
Thanks, Ari.
Half a sandwich later...
Bin:
Nice of you to share something as brilliant as this sandwich.

Aria:
Glad you liked it.

Bin:
I think I see your point about giving yourself some time off.

This is nicer than cramming work.

Aria:
The sunset looks cool as well.

Bin:
Don't stare at it too long or you'll go blind.

Aria:
As if you aren't the one with glasses!

Bin:
Those come from reading, not staring into the very thing they tell you to not.

Aria:
Alright, alright. You win this one.

'Bin the genius debator'.

Though, here's something you can't object to: going on a walk together.

Bin:
We're out here already, why not then?
2026 OCT 02 - Sunset Strolling
They end up talking about philosophy
