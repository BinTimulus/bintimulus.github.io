The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin

Dialogue 08 - 2026 NOV 15
Remember

2026 NOV 11, 15:52
Blackgrove College, Outside
(Bin and Aria are waling together out of school.)
Bin:
Eleven o'clock, eleventh of November.

That's an interesting chain of numbers.

Aria:
You talking about the World War One Armistice?

Bin:
Mhm. Which was today.

Got me thinking.

Aria:
As you always do-

(Bin stops walking abruptly, and stares at Aria, fed-up.)
Go on.

Bin:
As I was going to say:

Everyone blames the Archduke, but really, one Archduke dying having the ability to kill 22 million people just goes to show how horrifying alliance chains are. This, unfortunately, nobody remembers.

Nobody ever seems to remember the right things from history, do they, Ari?

Aria:
What do you mean?

Bin:
Everyone remembers 'war and atrocities bad', but not 'societal radicalisation and nationalistic rallying bad'; everyone remembers the side effects of vaccines, but not the countless horrors of diseases before those; everyone remembers financial crashes - but still, they invest everywhere recklessly.

Yes, of course, people dying in any way should not be forgotten, but what is actually wrong with our human brains?

Aria:
I'm getting lost here, Bin - you've shot your train of thought a little bit too far off for me to understand.

Bin:
We are so short-sighted, even towards the past, is what I mean.

Aria:
Oh. Like how everyone just thinks 'if I were in Nazi Germany I'd kill Hitler because I'd realise that he's a horrible person' before actually reading their history and realising that there is no way they would've thought of killing him because of all the propaganda he did before turning 'World War mode'?

Bin:
Something like that.

'We'll see it coming this time because we remember what happened in history,' they always say.

Aria:
And we still simplying print money at a financial crash every single time.

Bin:
Exactly.

Aria:
I guess our brains like a simple explanation, go for the most obvious cause for any event, and make the conclusion from that without ever considering that it runs much deeper than we'd like to think about.

Bin:
Remember the atrocities of the Japanese in the Pacific, but also remember the weaponisation fo religion that drove normal people to serve such a cause.

Remember the 22 million dead, but also remember the use of nationalism in society that led to explosive alliances.

Remember 9/11 dead, but remember that the Mujahideen was born of Cold War panic, which in itself was born of ideological rallying.

Remember each of them, and remember them all.

Aria:
There's never anything simple in life, is there?

Bin:
The Universe does not allow so.

Aria:
(Looks up) Damn you, Universe!

Bin:
That's the spirit.

Aria:
You know what, Bin?

Before this I thought you hated the idea of Rememberance.

I thought you'd see it as pointless.

Bin:
Remembering war deaths so we never make the same mistake again?

That is definitely not pointless.

It's just that we never remember enough to do anything to stop it from repeating.

Aria:
Colour me surprised.

Bin:
I guess everyone can surprise you however much you think you know about them, then.

Somewhat like this surprise of how we are short-sighted, even backwards.

Human history is a big. Flat. Circle. (Does a big circle with their hand while speaking.)

Aria:
(Chuckles) You look silly doing that.

Bin:
Who cares.
A bit of walking later...

Outside Stone St Park
Bin:
Ari (Taps her shoulder, then points) - poppies on a postbox.

Aria:
(Walks closer to take a look) These look nice.

First to grow after war on the Western Front, huh.

Bin:
Also the source of the drug that single-handedly collapsed dynastic China.

Aria:
And there you go talking about 'singular causes to events'.

Bin:
(Sighs) Think as I say, don't think as I think.
2026 NOV 11
Poppies on a Postbox
