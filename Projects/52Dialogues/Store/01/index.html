The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin

Dialogue 01: - 2026 SEP 23
Why celebrate birthdays?

2026 SEP 23, 17:30
Porton Cafe, Porton
(Bin enters with book in hand, and spots Aria and her usual afternoon sandwich.)

Bin:
I mean, by this point, how are you still not broke yet? Coffee - 3, sandwich - 3. Multiply that by 7...

Aria:
I don't come here every-

Bin:
Don't lie to me that you don't come here every day after school - that's 42 a week!

Aria:
Might I remind you that I, in fact, work Saturday mornings here - meaning that:
  1. I get employee's discounts, and;
  2. Unlike you, I have a source of income which offsets this.

Let me ask you instead. You come here every weekend as well to sit down and read about topics you won't use in a million years - sure, they sound cool and all everytime you talk about them - but how much time - and potential money - have you wasted on hoarding trivia instead of working?

Bin:
I mean...

Aria:
Exactly.

Though, you could say that I'm treating myself for my birthday. Sit! Let's chat! I haven't got plans!

Bin:
(Sits down, resting their book on the table.)
Your birthday, then? Don't expect a gift at such late notice. But no plans?

Aria:
I get the point of a small reward or celebration, but what's the point of a big celebration, anyways? Don't you get one day older every day, one second older every second? Why make the celebration of a year so much larger than that of a second then?

Bin:
A year is 365 times longer than a day, and 31.5 million times a second. So yes, there is a difference, isn't there?

Aria:
Then should I make a birthday party 31.5 million times larger than what I do everyday? That's unrealistic - and a waste of time!

Bin:
Sure, you can talk about scale, but this isn't just about the progression of your time on the Earth - don't you feel different waking up, knowing that you've existed for 31.5 million seconds longer?

Aria:
I don't go and throw a big party to make myself the big, big topic of the conversation of the day though. That's a bit intrusive, isn't it?

Bin:
But you still - or if you currently aren't, should still - see birthdays as special, right? I mean, if we stop giving arbitrary things, like a birthday, to a degree, meaning, to make them fun for no reason, this world would certainly become 31.5 million times more depressing.

Aria:
Fair enough.

Bin:
I get that neither of us are the "oh its my birthday let's call up the entire neighbourhood" type of person, but I see that we still set aside our birthdays to have fun, don't we?

Aria:
So ultimately, I do value birthdays just as much as those who I think blow it out of proportion - they simply take that sentiment and turn it into a big community event, and I, into time when I can have some peace with friends. We aren't as different as we think.

Bin:
Yep. We aren't as different as we think.

Talking about that...

(Gets up and proceeds to walk out of the Cafe, leaving their book on the table.)

Aria:
Talking about what? You can't just leave-

Bin:
In a minute - just going to get something!

And don't start eating your sandwich yet!
Five minutes later...
Bin:
(Returns to the Cafe, candles and a hat in hand.)

(Waves candle)
Let's try to see what all the big birthday party craze is about, Aria.

Aria:
Wait, Bin, what are you thinking of doing?

Bin:
Hmm... Let's see...
(Takes Aria's sandwich.)

Tada!
Aria:
What am I looking at.

Bin:
A cake and this both are carbohydrates. Not that much of a difference, is there?
Now put the hat on.

Aria:
Well. (Takes the hat, and puts it on)

Bin:
And...
