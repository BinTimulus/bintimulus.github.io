The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin

Dialogue 06 - 2026 OCT 30
Time Machines

2026 OCT 29, 16:35
Stone St Park, Porton
(Bin finds Aria leaning against the back of a bench, looking into the trees, waiting for them.)

Bin:
Hey Ari. Got your call.

(Bin sits on the bench, the right way round.)

Aria:
(Turns around to look at Bin.) Hey Bin.

Bin:
What's up, then?

Aria:
Not much, really.

I've just been thinking about a weird question:

What decision would you warn your past self against if you had a time machine?

Bin:
Probably going to that Christmas party in 2020.

Aria:
Oh. The covid one you told me about.

Makes sense.

Bin:
And you?

Aria:
Taking my bike to school.

Bin:
When you slid down a slope and into a wall three years ago?

Aria:
Yeah.

Breaking a bone does apparently hurt as much as advertised.

(She takes her left arm off the back of the bench, and looks down at it.)

At least I got to skip half the final that year...

Bin:
On the bright side.

Aria:
So much for that.

Bin:
So much for that indeed.

Aria:
But that isn't the full question.

Bin:
Oh?

Aria:
What really got me thinking was this.

So we fix that problem. What next?

Bin:
Not much. I'd end up catching covid one way or another in the end anyways.

Wait. That made my decision sound meaningless.

Aria:
And I'd probably mess up a few subjects.

I really wasn't revising that well that year.

And that made my decision sound pretty rubbish as well, when I thought about it.

Bin:
I see what's so interesting about this problem, then.

Whatever decision we change that gets us out of the immediate problem-

Aria:
Creates a hoard of new problems unforeseeable. Yes:

Bike to school. You break your arm - travel safer.
Walk to school. You mess up your exams - you should've studied.
Lock in for the exams. You burn out - start studying earlier.
Start studying earlier. You miss out on the fun - try balancing work and life.
Balance work and life. There is no balance - make work fun.
Try and find fun. Work can't be fun - change your mindset.
Embrace the grind. You lose yourself - don't embrace the grind.
Go for it chill. You fail - try harder.
Try locking in again. Burnout catches you again - there is no way out-
(Aria pauses, and looks into the distance.)

let go.
Let go: Mistakes make us. Don't fixate on fixing what's done.
(She sighs.) That's it. Let go.

Bin:
Oddly poetic, but true.

Stressing all day about how we could've done better doesn't help.

But this also doesn't mean that we should just slack off - we can't change where we were, but we can try and steer ourselves in a better direction.

How much we've learnt from time travel if we don't just keep killing Hitler.

Aria:
Yep. Funny how thinking about changing our pasts managed to teach us a life lesson.

Bin:
We do seem to always get something out of these weird conversations, don't we?

Aria:
Most productive thing I've done all term break.

Bin:
I'm sure you've done more.

You did revise, didn't you?

Aria:
I did, and I finished by homework.

Bin:
That was enough done then, wasn't it?

Aria:
As if you aren't reading ahead to the end of the syllabus this break.

Bin:
I'm not. I've just been reading loads of novels.

I'm not going to even remember what I read anyways.

(Aria hits Bin on the head, lightly.)

OW! What was that for?

Aria:
First world problems.

Bin:
As if complaining about even having free time to waste isn't one.

Aria:
Alright. Alright. You win.

(Turns around, still leaning on the back of the bench.)

Are you coming to the party on Saturday then?

Bin:
Probably.

But I don't think I'll be doing much there though.

(Bin proceeds to rummage through their bag, and takes a rubik's cube out.)

Aria:
Haven't seen you mess with one of these for a long time.

Can I mess it up for you?

Bin:
Again. Scrambling isn't called 'messing it up'.

Aria:
Whatever. Let me call it what I want.
2026 OCT 29
And Bin solves a Cube.
