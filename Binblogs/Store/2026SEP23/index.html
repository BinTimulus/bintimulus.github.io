A reflection on The 52 Dialogues of Aria and Bin - Dialogue 01

"Damn. This is cringe and feels like it was written by a political teenager who does not know what they are talking about."
C., 2026 SEP 17

My first post!
Let's do the context first. This is my first post on this site (if you haven't read it yet, go read it and ruin your day with slop), and maybe with this identity/persona.

I haven't really written anything since The Novel-Length Piece of Text that shall not be MentionedTM (more on that at a later date), which was about two years ago. So thank you for reading this. And please, leave a comment or some feedback (other than that mentioned above) in the Insta comments section!
Why?
Now that we've got the trivial stuff out of the way, it's time for the reasons for this text.

For writing this series, I really only decided to start this on a whim, wanting to write something and put it online for people to read (I am, ultimately, very selfish), and for myself to put down whatever chaotic mess of ideas I have held in my brain for the week or so into text. In fact, I created this whole website because of this whim. Thus, don't read too deeply into anything on here.

And for this particular dialogue? A birthday of a family member is around this period, and it reminded me that unlike many people I know, our family rarely does big birthday parties, even for big years like the 18th or so, and so I decided to write something about it!
What about the production?
I wrote this in about three days, drew something in MS Paint for the funsies, and sent it off to proof- and re-reading with C., a harsh yet genuine critic. He told me the exact quote above, yet oddly still advised me to upload this because apparently "We all need a little cringe in our lives".

"It's my first piece," I thought, "And it's anonymous, so why not?"

So I uploaded this thing. I will probably read back on this and cringe so hard my face implodes, but we all gotta start somewhere, right? (Maybe some of us shouldn't start at all.)
Any extra thoughts?
You can very obviously see that my thoughts are trailing off by this point and what I'm writing is becoming more and more fragmented. We go on nonetheless.

One. If you can't tell, the whole ending part was written to lead up to the funny "birthday sandwich" picture, which, in retrospect, wasn't really that funny. Got to practice my long abandoned art skills though! So I guess that did mean something.

Two. I only realised as I was writing this that I forgot to mention anything about having your birthday be on the weekdays being an absolutely terrible experience for anyone who does birthdays with plans at night, because those plans can't stretch too late or you risk not being able to wake up on time the next day. So that's that.

Three. About the setting itself. Porton isn't real. I know that there is a place in England called that, but I kind of only realised after I decided that this was the best generic place name. Aria and Bin are also just names that I stumbled upon by chance (woah, I am compelled by spontaneity!) that just so happened to fit an "Alice and Bob" situation, and their characterisations aren't really that well though out yet. (Put this awful writer in jail for malpractice!) Though, I guess you could say that the names are individually special to me for separate reasons.
The conclusion?
Woe, the corny mention of the celebration of 31.5 million seconds passing be upon ye!


Aside from that? Thanks for reading, go give some feedback maybe, and I hope that I didn't make your day actually any worse. See you next week! Holy this was a hell of a rewrite.
