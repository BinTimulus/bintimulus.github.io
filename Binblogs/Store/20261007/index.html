To begin?
I am, ultimately, the most free on the weekend, and for the past two weeks chasing a Wednesday upload deadline has not yielded any benefits for myself. I am writing this on the Tuesday afternoon before upload - this simply goes to show how much trouble I'm in.

And thus? I'm moving my uploads to Fridays so I can actually start work on the weekends as soon as I finish each upload and ride off the working momentum. This means that you won't see an upload from me until 9 days later, not a week.
I know, I know. What a selfish author.
About this piece?
How you allocate your work and rest seems like a basic enough topic to write about, so why not?

Though, here's a bit more about how I'm connected to this discussion. I'm Bin. I choose to cram all my work first every day and weekend before allowing myself any rest. I end up feeling miserable every night. So maybe I should take on my own advice of spreading my work out, but again, it's a tight balance between distributing tasks evenly and falling victim to procrastination. I'm repeating myself, am I not?
